How this works

TD% is the model's probability the player scores at least one rushing or receiving touchdown (QBs: rushing only). It starts from expected touchdowns per game: every carry and target over the last 5 weeks (60%) and season-to-date (40%), each weighted by how often that kind of look scores league-wide (by yard line, and whether a target was thrown into the end zone). That is adjusted for this week's Vegas implied team total versus the team's own scoring so far, and for the opponent's red-zone TD rate allowed, then recalibrated against the previous season's actual results.

Backtest (2024 to 2026, 7,800 player-games, never calibrated on the season being tested): each tenth of predictions landed within about 3 points of the real scoring rate, except the lowest group (6% predicted vs 9% actual). That beats a flat base-rate guess by about 7% on Brier score. That's a real but modest edge; touchdowns are noisy.

Score (0 to 100) ranks players within the week: 35% high-leverage opportunities (inside-10 carries and targets, end-zone targets, weighted by TD value), 25% share of the team's red-zone opportunities, 25% implied team total, 15% opponent red-zone TD rate allowed. In the backtest, the top fifth by score scored 40% of the time and the bottom fifth 11%.

Book is the consensus anytime-TD price from The Odds API, updated Tuesday afternoon and Friday night. Most books only post the "Yes" side, so that probability still contains the book's margin; Edge (TD% minus book%) is therefore conservative. Fair is the model's TD% as American odds.

Players ruled Out are removed; Questionable and Doubtful players are shown at a full workload, with a badge. A "No recent games" badge means the player hasn't played in the last 5 weeks (injury, inactive, or a lost role), so his TD% rests on earlier-season usage that may no longer apply; treat it skeptically. Games already played that week drop off. Rookies and players with fewer than 2 games this season aren't scored.
